## 🛠️ Projects

### 📊 Creator Deal Desk (CPM · ROI · ROAS)
A single-file dashboard that replaces a multi-tab ROI workbook for pricing creator partnerships.
- Calculates effective CPM across **YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and blogs**, with outlier filtering on views
- Works in reverse: paste a creator's rate card and get the real CPM to accept or counter
- Funnel model (reach → engagement → leads → sales → revenue) with pessimistic, realistic and optimistic long-tail scenarios
- Multi-currency (EUR, AUD, USD, GBP), 1 to 12 month packages, seasonal content calendar export

`HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript` · [▶ Live demo](https://fabriziodemel-stack.github.io/creator-deal-desk/) · [Code](https://github.com/fabriziodemel-stack/creator-deal-desk)

### 🗂️ Partner Command Center
A daily operations cockpit for partnership managers.
- Pulls together **Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Impact and Google Workspace**
- Builds a prioritised task list every morning, with reply drafts ready to send
- Every metric (ROAS, ROI, CR) shows its working, so numbers are always explainable

`HTML` `JavaScript` `Google Apps Script` `APIs` · [▶ Live demo](https://fabriziodemel-stack.github.io/partner-command-center/) · [Code](https://github.com/fabriziodemel-stack/partner-command-center)
